# AI Models

This document provides a conceptual overview of the artificial intelligence models used within the AEROSONIC Sonifier — AI Framework.  
No model architectures, parameters, training data or implementation details are disclosed.

---

## Purpose of AI Models in the System

AI models in the AEROSONIC ecosystem serve to:

- interpret environmental patterns  
- support probabilistic decision‑making  
- generate emergent musical behaviour  
- mediate transitions between musical states  
- enhance adaptivity and responsiveness  

Their role is conceptual rather than explicitly technical in this documentation.

---

## Conceptual Model Categories

### 1. Pattern Interpretation Models

Used by the **Weather Agent** and **Anomaly Detector** to:

- recognise environmental tendencies  
- detect deviations from typical behaviour  
- classify meteorological states  

These models conceptually transform raw data into meaningful environmental information.

---

### 2. Generative Behaviour Models

Used by the **Dreaming AI** to:

- explore alternative trajectories  
- introduce variation and imagination  
- generate emergent behaviour  

These models conceptually expand the expressive space of the system.

---

### 3. Probabilistic Transition Models

Used by the **Quantum State Agent** to:

- manage stochastic transitions  
- weight possible musical states  
- regulate unpredictability  

They conceptually govern the system’s fluidity and evolution.

---

### 4. Musical Behaviour Models

Used by the **Harmonic Field Agent** and **Rhythm Engine** to:

- shape harmonic regions  
- define rhythmic tendencies  
- maintain musical coherence  

These models conceptually translate environmental conditions into musical behaviour.

---
