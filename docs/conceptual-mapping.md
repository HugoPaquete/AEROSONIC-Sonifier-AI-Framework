# Conceptual Mapping

This document describes the conceptual strategies used to map meteorological information into musical behaviour within the AEROSONIC Sonifier — AI Framework.  
The mappings presented here are abstract and do not reveal implementation details, algorithms, or parameter values.

---

## Purpose of Conceptual Mapping

Conceptual mapping defines how:

- environmental conditions influence musical states  
- agents interpret and transform information  
- transitions occur between behaviours  
- the system maintains coherence while remaining adaptive  

These mappings serve as design principles rather than technical specifications.

---

## 1. Environmental → Behavioural Mapping

Meteorological data is conceptually transformed into behavioural tendencies.  
Examples include:

- **Wind intensity → rhythmic density**  
- **Temperature variation → harmonic tension**  
- **Atmospheric pressure shifts → structural transitions**  
- **Humidity gradients → timbral or textural tendencies**  

These relationships are conceptual and do not imply direct parameter control.

---

## 2. Behavioural → Musical Mapping

The AI agents generate high‑level musical directives, such as:

- harmonic region  
- rhythmic mode  
- tempo tendency  
- expressive intensity  
- probabilistic triggers  

These directives are interpreted by the musical output layer to shape the sonification.

---

## 3. State‑Based Mapping

The system operates through conceptual states, such as:

- **Stable** — predictable environmental conditions  
- **Transitional** — shifting or unstable conditions  
- **Anomalous** — rare or unexpected events  
- **Exploratory** — Dreaming AI influence  
- **Collapsed** — Quantum State Agent resolution  

Each state influences the behaviour of the agents and the resulting musical output.

---

## 4. Multi‑Agent Influence Mapping

The agents interact conceptually through influence rather than direct control:

- **Weather Agent → Harmonic Field Agent**  
  Environmental intensity shapes harmonic behaviour.

- **Anomaly Detector → State Machine**  
  Rare events trigger structural transitions.

- **Dreaming AI ↔ Quantum State Agent**  
  Imagination and probability balance each other.

- **Harmonic Field Agent ↔ Rhythm Engine**  
  Ensures musical coherence across layers.

This distributed mapping supports emergent behaviour.

---

## 5. Temporal Mapping

The system evolves over time through:

- gradual transitions  
- probabilistic shifts  
- environmental modulation  
- agent‑driven exploration  

Time is treated as a dynamic dimension rather than a fixed sequence.

---

## 6. Conceptual Mapping Principles

The framework follows several guiding principles:

- **Abstraction** — mappings describe tendencies, not parameters.  
- **Emergence** — behaviour arises from agent interaction.  
- **Adaptivity** — the system responds continuously to environmental change.  
- **Non‑linearity** — mappings are not direct or deterministic.  
- **Protection** — no implementation details are disclosed.
